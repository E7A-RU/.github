# GOV-AUTHZ-001 — E7A Centralized Authorization Boundary

**Decision:** OWNER-APPROVED ARCHITECTURAL DIRECTION (2026-11-10). **Document release status:** proposed normative change; current immutable Governance v2.1.0 (revision `c26fc1daea258a8ba63b61ea069664f6d9a51da2`) remains in force until a separately approved, immutable follow-up standards release. No production changes, enrollment or privileges are authorized by this ADR.

## Problem and superseded boundary

Current Governance 2.1.0 divides Identity (authentication, membership, entitlements) and product-owned resource ACL. The Owner explicitly selects **central E7A authority for ecosystem authorization, resource grants, policy schemas, delegation and trust**, preserving independent product code, domain data and releases. This is an intentional normative change, not proof that existing products are migrated. Where earlier documents say product ACL or Platform connector-specific permission is the *sole* authorization authority, read as **transitional runtime state** pending independent adoption; those product-side checks remain required as enforcement and domain invariants.

## Binding architectural contract after new standards release

1. **E7A Identity is the central authority** for Person, Account linking, SecurityActor, ServicePrincipal, canonical Organization, Workspace, Product Trust Registry, Product Administration Grants, Organization Entitlements, Product Installations, reusable Policy Templates/Instances and ecosystem resource grants/relations. No authorization solely from a username, email, self-asserted Workspace, shared cookie, or IdP group.
2. **Product remains independent**: its own DB, business resource lifecycle, owner-specific data, validation and application-side Policy Enforcement Point. Product declares versioned resource types, actions and proposed role compositions through a trust-reviewed Product Manifest. The E7A Identity Policy Control Plane approves and publishes schema/model versions; product applies checked authorization and business invariants. Existing product ACL remains active until tested migration, not silently replaced by a proposed service.
3. **External provider choices** (ZITADEL OIDC/SSO/MFA/federation and OpenFGA relationships with an E7A Policy Runtime for entitlements, risk and assurance) are selected for initial **Identity implementation** under its owner-approved architecture and vendor-specific security/license/Infra PoC gates; they are not mandatory dependencies for anonymous products, Core, or other E7A runtimes. Replacement requires controlled ADR. Do not turn vendor Organization into canonical E7A Organization or implement custom OAuth cryptography.
4. **Registration and trust are separate gates.** Registration assigns immutable opaque product ID, independent of mutable name/slug. An approved manifest, versioned grants schema, OAuth/service credentials, installation approval and permission to deploy are **different**, auditable, revocable decisions. No naming reservations or automatic platform roles based on product labels.
5. **Personal Workspace at registration; Organization separately created** with quotas/anti-abuse. Person may belong to multiple Organizations and Workspaces; memberships are independent of grants. E7A itself is an Organization; platform root/Infra/keys and break-glass are separate high-privilege boundaries. Corporate Organization, legal existence, verified Person, representation and authentication assurance are distinct evidence claims.
6. **Installation v1:** one owning Organization per Product Installation, **several explicitly authorized Workspaces owned by that Organization** within versioned `workspace_scope`. Multiple installations of one product are isolated; cross-Organization shared collaboration uses explicit scoped and revocable grants, **not co-owned installation** in v1. Resource and organization/installation selectors from clients never establish authority.
7. **Entitlement/delegation ceilings:** Product Schema ∩ active Organization Entitlement ∩ active Installation/Workspace scope ∩ delegated role/grants ∩ central safety conditions. An Organization admin may create roles only by composing approved permissions within a delegation ceiling. Default `user` role applies only to an explicitly authorized installation, not all registered products.
8. **Product technical owners/maintainers/support may come from outside the owning Organization.** Grant explicit narrow Product Administration capabilities (support has no default customer data access). Non-escalating compatible schema changes may proceed under bounded risk-approved automation; sensitive capability expansion, tenant boundary/key changes and privileged role changes need separate Owner/security review. Identity/platform release rules still apply.
9. **Policy reuse:** version-pinned managed templates with impact review, independent copy/fork allowing local maintenance, and tightly controlled live inheritance. Fork never copies user data, grants or secrets; global mandatory security restrictions cannot be removed by a fork.
10. **Central decision does not remove backend enforcement.** Products register resource IDs and relationships through authorized idempotent outbox/reconciliation, preserving a pending-deny state on failed registration. Independent DB transactions are not globally atomic. Never claim an entitlement alone allows editing a particular object.
11. **Continuity and revoke:** R0 anonymous/offline does not depend on Identity. R1 narrowly scoped signed authorization leases up to 30 seconds only with sufficiently fresh, gap-free revocation state; R2/R3 fresh online authorization, fail closed, step-up if required. Under network isolation immediate global revocation cannot be guaranteed. Healthy propagation targets 5s (administrative revoke) and 15s (ordinary grants) require measurement before becoming SLA. Failures cannot fall back to indefinite snapshots.
12. **E7A Core** remains versioned optional libraries, UI/design tokens and optional Product Starter. Future identity React/server SDK and conformance checks follow approved E7A brand/design. Never central shared backend, no GitHub Actions hosted CI or forced auth on Website/local Reader.
13. **No release/security gate relaxation.** Exact-SHA evidence, segregated infra credentials, least privilege, risk review, individual Identity R2+ approval, private test data and backups/restore, privacy/KYC/KYB controls, operator break-glass independent of a failed IdP, and product-specific rollout/rollback remain mandatory. Governance source merge is not the standards release, installation, runtime test or deployment permission.

## Migration and precedence

- Before the **new immutable Governance release**, version 2.1.0 remains normative. This ADR and owner decision are approved **direction** but a product-specific implementation may not override contradictory 2.1.0 requirements by claiming this draft is normative.
- Publish a *new* versioned Governance release through normal source-merge → immutable SHA → separately reviewed descriptor procedure; do not mutate/relabel previously published 2.1.0. Version number (minor vs major) must be evaluated with backward-compatibility and consumer impact.
- Adopt in each consumer via independent reviewed PR: `governance-consumer.json` pins exact new revision, records compatibility and risk; no bulk push, auto-merge or deployment. Existing product ACL/tenant security is retained until central checks are verified in dual-read/shadow/negative tests, cutover rollback and per-product authorization.
- Authorization APIs and SDK are **not** mandatory startup dependency of anonymous website, local Reader, or currently running services before their own approved migration.
- Identity internal ADRs define exact ZITADEL/OpenFGA schema/protocol, compiled policy boundaries, leases and tests, subject to this higher-level standard.

## Compatibility / risk checklist

- **MUST preserve:** independent product business DB/release, B2C anonymous entry where present, Core as optional library/Starter, risk-gated self-hosted QA and deployment, protected audit, separate operator approval, release rollback.
- **MUST test:** cross-tenant and cross-installation access, grant amplification, Technical Owner external delegation, mutable product name, template fork updates, resource/entitlement revocation, stale policy cache vs R1 lease, down IdP/PDP/OpenFGA and emergency operations, user migration and consent.
- **MUST document:** data residence/privacy, technical product operator separation, authN/AuthZ boundaries, ZITADEL AGPL/license review, backup/key ownership, resource registry reconciliation and decommission.

**Owner decision:** authorized to prepare new Governance normative source and impact proposal; not authority to bypass publication checks, produce a self-approved release descriptor or deploy E7A Identity.
